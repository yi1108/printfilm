Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

Shenzhen Depei Open Source Data Technology Co., Ltd. (“PrintFilm,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) respects your privacy. This policy explains how we collect, use, and share information when you use the PrintFilm iOS app, https://www.printfilm.com, and related generation, playback, and account services (the “Service”).

If you do not agree with this policy, do not use the Service. Our Terms of Use explain the rules for using PrintFilm.

1. Who is responsible

The controller of your personal information is Shenzhen Depei Open Source Data Technology Co., Ltd. You can contact us at support@printfilm.com.

2. Information we collect

We collect the following categories of information, depending on how you use the Service.

Account information. When you register or sign in, we collect your email address, password (stored by us only in a protected form), and display name. If you add them, we also collect an optional phone number and a profile photo. We keep an account identifier so we can associate your credits, generations, and purchases with you.

Guest identifier. If you use Watch or sharing without an account, the app creates a random identifier on the device and may send it with a share event so we can count that share. It is not used to track you across other companies’ apps or websites.

Content you submit. We collect text prompts, photos or reference images you choose to upload, comments, captions, likes, reports, and videos or images you ask us to generate or publish. If you save a result to Photos, that copy stays on your device under your control.

Voice. If you dictate a prompt or use a voice feature, speech is converted to text on your device with Apple’s speech recognition. We receive the resulting text if you submit it as a prompt or message. We do not receive a recording from that on-device transcription unless a feature you start clearly sends audio to us.

On-device Studio data. After you unlock Studio, Assistant, Voice, and Music run on your iPhone. If you ask the assistant to use them, the app may access your microphone, photos, contacts, calendars, reminders, or location. Those permissions are requested by iOS for that action. We do not upload your contacts, calendar, or reminders merely because the permission exists.

Purchases and usage. We receive records of credit packs and the Studio unlock so we can deliver what you bought. Apple processes the payment. We do not receive your full payment-card number. We keep credit balance, holds, generation charges, token usage, unlocks of paid videos, and purchase history for the account.

Device and log data. We collect device model, operating-system version, app version, language, IP address, and timestamps. We use this to deliver the Service, keep accounts signed in, prevent abuse, and diagnose failures.

Support messages. If you email support@printfilm.com, we keep the message and the contact details you send, including information you choose to include about a problem.

We do not sell personal information. We do not use Apple’s App Tracking Transparency framework, because we do not track you across other companies’ apps and websites for advertising. We do not show third-party advertising in the app.

3. How we use information

We use information to:

  • Create and secure your account, and let you sign in;
  • Generate images and videos from prompts and files you submit, and show them in your library;
  • Operate Watch, playback of free, preview, and purchased versions, comments, likes, and shares;
  • Sell and deliver credits and Studio access, settle usage, and show balance and history;
  • Moderate content, investigate reports, and enforce our Terms of Use;
  • Answer support requests;
  • Measure performance, fix defects, and prevent fraud, abuse, and security incidents;
  • Comply with law and respond to lawful requests.

We do not use your content to sell advertising profiles. We may use prompts, uploads, and outputs to operate safety filters and to investigate abuse. We do not sell those materials.

4. Legal bases for people in the EEA, the UK, and Switzerland

Where the GDPR, UK GDPR, or Swiss data-protection law applies, we rely on:

  • Contract. Providing the account, generation, playback, credits, and support you request.
  • Legitimate interests. Securing the Service, preventing fraud, moderating harmful content, understanding aggregate reliability, and improving features, balanced against your rights. You may object as described below.
  • Consent. Where iOS asks you for access to the microphone, speech recognition, photos, contacts, calendars, reminders, or location, and where we ask for consent for an optional use. You can withdraw a device permission in iOS Settings.
  • Legal obligation. Keeping records we must keep, and responding to lawful requests.

5. AI processing

Cloud image and video generation sends your prompt, the settings for that request, and any photo you choose to include to our servers and to infrastructure and model providers that process the request for us. Those providers may handle the material only so they can perform the generation, store it for the period we agree for delivery and safety, and must not use it for their own advertising.

Outputs are produced automatically. They can be wrong or resemble other content. Review them before you publish or rely on them. Do not submit secrets, or personal information you do not want processed for generation, in a prompt or reference image.

On-device Studio tools process the request on your iPhone. A prompt you later send to cloud generation is handled as cloud content, even if you first composed it on the device.

6. When we share information

We share personal information with:

  • Cloud hosting, storage, and AI processing providers, under contract, and only to run the Service;
  • Apple, when you make an in-app purchase, restore a purchase, or use an Apple framework such as speech recognition or the photo library. Apple’s privacy policy describes Apple’s own processing;
  • Other users, when you publish a video, comment, like, or share, to the extent your display name, profile photo, and content are shown;
  • Professional advisers, and authorities or other parties, when we believe disclosure is required by law or necessary to protect users, the public, or our rights;
  • A buyer or successor, if we reorganize or sell the Service, in which case we will continue to protect the information as described here or notify you of a material change.

We do not share personal information with third parties for their own advertising, and we do not sell or “share” personal information as those words are used in the California Consumer Privacy Act.

7. International transfers

We are based in Shenzhen, China. We and our service providers may process information in China and in other countries where we or they operate. Those countries may not provide the same level of protection as your home country.

Where the GDPR or UK GDPR requires a transfer tool, we use an approved mechanism, such as the European Commission’s standard contractual clauses and the UK addendum or International Data Transfer Agreement, together with safeguards appropriate to the transfer. You may ask for a summary of the relevant safeguard by emailing support@printfilm.com.

8. How long we keep information

We keep account information, content, and purchase records while your account is open and you use the Service. After you close your account, we delete or irreversibly anonymize personal information within a reasonable period, normally within 30 days, except where we need to retain specific records for longer:

  • Purchase, refund, and credit records, for tax, accounting, and fraud prevention, typically for up to 7 years or the period required by law;
  • Safety reports, moderation records, and security logs, for as long as needed to investigate abuse and protect other users;
  • Information in backups, until the backup cycle overwrites them, after which they are deleted in the ordinary course;
  • Content you made public, which may persist in another user’s copy, a share preview, or a backup until it is removed in the ordinary course.

Guest share identifiers are kept only as long as needed to count and deduplicate share events.

9. Security

We use encryption in transit (HTTPS) and access controls designed to limit who can see personal information. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure. Use a unique password and keep your device locked.

10. Your choices and rights

In the app. You can review and edit your display name, phone number, and profile photo in your account. You can close your account at Me → Settings → Close Account. Closing an email account requires your password and the confirmation word shown on that screen. Closure signs you out and is a request to delete the account and the personal information described above.

Device permissions. You can turn off microphone, speech recognition, photos, contacts, calendars, reminders, and location access in iOS Settings. If you turn a permission off, the feature that needs it will not work.

Access, correction, deletion, and export. Depending on where you live, you may have the right to ask for a copy of your personal information, to correct it, to delete it, to restrict or object to certain processing, and to receive information you provided in a portable format. You may withdraw consent where processing is based on consent, without affecting processing that took place before withdrawal.

EEA, UK, and Switzerland. You may lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority. We ask you to contact us first so we can try to resolve the issue.

California and other U.S. states. Residents may have the right to know, delete, and correct personal information, and to appeal a denial. We do not sell personal information and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. We do not discriminate against you for exercising these rights. We will not ask you to verify your identity in a way that is more burdensome than the request requires. You may use an authorized agent where the law allows; we may still ask you to confirm the request.

To use any of these rights, email support@printfilm.com from the address on your account, or use Close Account in the app for deletion. We may need to confirm that the request comes from you. We will respond within the time required by applicable law, generally within 30 days, and we will tell you if we need more time.

11. Children

PrintFilm is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has given us personal information, email support@printfilm.com and we will delete it. People under 18 should use the Service only with a parent or guardian’s consent.

12. Changes to this policy

We will post updates at https://www.printfilm.com/privacy and change the “Last updated” date. If a change materially affects how we use personal information, we will provide a clearer notice, such as in the app or by email, where that is reasonable. The version on the website is the current policy.
